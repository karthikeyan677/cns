Readme / viva noteHow does Caesar Cipher work?

A Caesar Cipher moves every letter through the alphabet by the same number of places. A key of 3 turns A into D, B into E, and so on. When the alphabet ends, the shift wraps back to its beginning. Spaces, numbers, and punctuation are never moved.

E(x) = (x + k) mod 26 encrypt / move forward
D(x) = (x − k) mod 26 decrypt / move backward
